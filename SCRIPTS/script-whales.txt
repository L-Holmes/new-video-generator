Here's the thing. In the middle of the Sahara sits a valley called Wadi Al-Hitan. Scientists digging there found ribs, vertebrae, teeth, and entire skulls. Whale skulls. The ground looked as if an ocean had simply dried up around them. Every fossil was intact except one. Locals swear the wind sounds like whale song at night. And then boom, a sandstorm buried the site for a decade. Recovering it cost about $2 million.

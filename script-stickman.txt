
If you open your kitchen cupboard right now, you probably have a jar
of nutmeg. 
It costs about two dollars.
But in the 1600s, this little wrinkled seed was the single most contested resource on the planet.
It was worth more than its weight in gold.

Nutmeg only grew in one place on Earth:
The Banda Islands. A tiny, incredibly remote volcanic archipelago in modern-day Indonesia.

If you were a European merchant, getting it meant
sailing for months, surviving 
scurvy, 
pirates,
and shipwrecks. But if you made it back with a 
single ship full of nutmeg, you were set for life. 
The Dutch and the British fought bloody, brutal wars over these tiny islands.

In fact, to secure a monopoly on nutmeg, 
the Dutch formally traded a different island they owned to the British.

That island was Manhattan. Yep... New York City was traded for nutmeg.

A flat white costs about four pounds. Buy one every working day and you hand over £1,000 a year. Invest the same money instead and after thirty years it is worth £79,000. Every pound of that pot splits three ways: 45% from the money you put in, 35% from growth, and 20% from the growth on the growth. Here is the part nobody mentions. 68% of the whole thing arrives in the final decade. The first ten years look like nothing is happening at all. That is the test, and most people quit at year four.

Skip Rome. Fly into Bologna instead. It sits 40 minutes from Florence, 90 minutes from Venice, and nobody is there. The old centre is wrapped in porticoes: 38 kilometres of covered walkway, so it can pour with rain and you never get wet. Eat tagliatelle al ragu, not spaghetti bolognese, which does not exist here. Climb the Asinelli tower at sunset. That is 498 steps. Then walk back down and drink a spritz for three euros.

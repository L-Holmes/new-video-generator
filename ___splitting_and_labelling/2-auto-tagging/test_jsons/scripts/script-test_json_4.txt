I got fired on a Tuesday. Not laid off. Fired. My manager called me into the small room with the broken blind, said the words, and stared at his hands the whole time. I walked out to my car, sat there, and did not cry. Then I drove to the beach and ate an entire bag of crisps. Two years later, that company folded. I am not saying that was my doing. I am saying the crisps were incredible.

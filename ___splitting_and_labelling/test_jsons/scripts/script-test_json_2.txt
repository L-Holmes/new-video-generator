Stop chopping your garlic. Here's the thing. Everything you love about garlic bread comes from three things: butter, salt, and time. Take one whole head, slice the top clean off, and drown it in olive oil. Ninety minutes in the oven at 150 degrees. It comes out golden, sweet, and soft enough to spread with a spoon. Squeeze the cloves straight onto warm sourdough. That is it. No pan, no chopping board, no crying.

The immortal jellyfish is roughly the size of your fingernail. When it is starving, injured, or simply old, it does something no other animal on Earth can do. It turns back into a baby. The cells rewind, the tentacles retract, and it sinks to the seafloor as a polyp. Then it grows all the way up again. Researchers in Kyoto watched a single one do this fourteen times. It is not truly immortal. It is just very, very hard to finish.

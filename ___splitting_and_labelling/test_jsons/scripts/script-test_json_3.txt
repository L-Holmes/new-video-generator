In 1996, a rookie guard shot 511 three pointers and missed 375 of them. Every coach in the league told him to stop. He shot more the next season. Attempts across the league grew from 9,000 a year in 1996 to 24,000 by 2016. Today, 39% of every shot taken comes from behind the arc. The game did not quietly evolve. One stubborn player dragged it, kicking and screaming, into a completely different sport.

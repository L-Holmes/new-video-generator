In 1518, a woman stepped into a street in Strasbourg and began to dance. She did not stop. Within a week, 34 people had joined her. Within a month, there were 400. The city's answer was to hire musicians, on the theory that the dancers simply needed to get it out of their system. People danced until they collapsed. Some of them died. Nobody has ever explained it. The best guess we have involves mouldy bread.
